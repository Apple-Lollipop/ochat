Keyboard shortcuts

Global combinations work outside Ochat. Record any key, with or without modifiers. For Alt or Ctrl alone, press and release the key; left and right are distinct. Modifier-only combinations such as Ctrl + Left Alt are supported. They activate once when a key is released; adding another key cancels that gesture. You can also type a combination manually. Privacy

Encrypted connections

Your invitation pins a peer’s public identity and carries the room access secret. QUIC encrypts messages, media and files between the participants. Peers can learn each other’s IP address. Optional relays see routing metadata, not message contents.

Encrypted local history

Your password unlocks your saved profile, rooms, messages and emojis. Export an encrypted backup and keep the private recovery key separately to move to another device. Your key alone cannot recreate lost data.

Your choices

Contact IDs share your public identity and connection addresses with the people you choose. They are not published to a public address directory. A friend request must be accepted before opening a private chat.

The microphone starts only after you accept a private call, join group voice or explicitly start a local test. Room members can share invitations and edit the shared emoji library. Anyone in a conversation can record it.

Limits

Downloaded files are saved unencrypted at your chosen location. Ochat does not protect a compromised device or system memory dumps. There is no offline message delivery or automatic device synchronization. This release is not independently audited and is not compatible with Tox or Ochat 0.1.
